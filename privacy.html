Privacy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This website

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security (GitHub's statement).

Purchases

Payments are handled by Stripe. At checkout, Stripe collects your name, email address, payment details and the billing address details the form asks for, such as country and postcode. Card details go to Stripe; we never see your full card number or security code.

For each payment, we receive and keep this from Stripe:

  • your name, email address, billing address details, and phone number if you gave one;
  • your card's brand, type (credit or debit), issuing country, last 4 digits and expiry date, whether you paid with a wallet such as Apple Pay, and an identifier Stripe uses to recognise the same card;
  • the amount, currency and date, any refund or dispute, the results of Stripe's fraud and card checks, the payment's reference numbers, and a link to your receipt.

We use this to deliver your order, send receipts, handle refunds, and keep legal and tax records. We don't sell it, and we share it with no one but Stripe unless the law requires it.

We keep these records as long as tax law requires. To ask us to delete your data, email timothy@waldin.net.

Stripe's own use of your data is covered by Stripe's privacy policy.
